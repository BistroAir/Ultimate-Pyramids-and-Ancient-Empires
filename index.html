Pantheon & Piazza Navona
🏭 History📷 Photography
The Pantheon (€5 entry, book at pantheonroma.com) — the best-preserved ancient Roman building in the world, with the largest unreinforced concrete dome ever built. Piazza Navona is 5 min walk — Bernini's Fountain of the Four Rivers. Best in early morning.

Trastevere & Campo de' Fiori Market
🚶 Walking🏪 Market
Trastevere is Rome's most atmospheric neighborhood — cobblestone lanes, ivy-covered buildings, excellent restaurants. Campo de' Fiori hosts a morning market (Mon–Sat, 7 AM–2 PM) with fresh produce, flowers, and local food. Combine both in a morning walk.

Fish Market (Mercato Ittico)

🏭Activities & Sights
Haute Ville (Citadel) — Medieval Old Town
🚶 Walking📷 Photography
Founded 830 AD — one of Corsica's most atmospheric medieval towns perched on soaring white limestone cliffs. Walk Porte de Gênes, Sainte-Marie-Majeure church, Bastion de l'Etendard viewpoint, and the Marine Cemetery. The view from the citadel walls over the strait toward Sardinia is extraordinary.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
King of Aragon's Staircase
🏭 History📷 Photography
187 steps carved directly into the cliff face — legend says cut in a single night during the 1420 Aragonese siege. Descend from the Old Town to a rocky ledge above the sea. Views back up to the citadel are extraordinary. Strenuous — wear sturdy shoes.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
Cliff & Sea Cave Boat Tour
✓ Holly📷 Photography
The essential Bonifacio experience — circle the limestone cliffs by boat, drift through the Sdragonato sea cave (ceiling hole shaped like Corsica) and marvel at the citadel from the water. 60–90 min. Book at marina kiosks as soon as you tender — fills fast in season.
📍 Marina
Lavezzi Islands — Snorkeling
✓ Holly
30–40 min boat ride to the Lavezzi Islands nature reserve — granite islets, turquoise lagoons, some of the clearest water in the Mediterranean. 3–4 hours. Book at the marina. April water is cool but crystal clear.
📍 Map
Capo Pertusato Coastal Path
🚶 Walking
3 km each way from Col Saint-Roch at the top of the old town — follows limestone cliffs to the southernmost tip of Corsica with sweeping views of the strait and Sardinia. Easy–moderate. 1.5–2 hrs each way.
📍 Map
🍽Food & Wine
Holly-safe: Corsican seafood — grilled fish, langoustines, sea urchin (oursin). Say "sans ail" (no garlic). Corsican Vermentino (white) and Muscat are excellent. Avoid charcuterie (pork-heavy). Chestnut-based products are dairy-free.
Eat in the Haute Ville — Not the Marina
🐟 Seafood
Marina restaurants are convenient but pricier and more touristy. Climb up and find a table in the citadel streets for better food at fairer prices. Look for restaurants serving Corsican specialties — fresh fish, chestnut dishes.
Corsican Wines
🍷 Wine
For Holly: Corsican Vermentino (crisp, mineral, excellent with seafood), Muscat du Cap Corse (sweet white — aperitif style), Patrimonio Blanc. Look for Domaine Orenga de Gaffory and Clos Canarelli labels.

Porto Cervo Marina & Piazzetta

San Pantaleo Village Market
🏪 Market✓ Holly
15 min by taxi inland — a charming hilltop village with a Thursday and Sunday market. Local Sardinian products: olive oil, bottarga (mullet roe — a Holly-safe delicacy), honey, ceramics. The village square has excellent local restaurants at a fraction of Porto Cervo prices.
📍 Map
Piccolo Pevero Beach
✓ Holly📷 Photography
Early swim in glass-clear water before the first tender arrives — the classic Porto Cervo experience. Short taxi or walk from the marina. In April the water is cool but the beach is empty and the light is extraordinary.
📍 Map
Maddalena Archipelago — by Boat
✓ Holly
An hour north by boat — granite islets, turquoise lagoons, and water that photographs like the Caribbean. Day boats run from Porto Cervo marina. The Scenic Ikon's Zodiacs may offer excursions here — check with the ship's Discovery Team.
📍 Map
🍽Food & Wine
Holly-safe: Bottarga (mullet roe — excellent on pasta or alone), fresh grilled fish, seafood pasta, Sardinian flatbread pane carasau (dairy-free). Say "senza aglio" (no garlic). Vermentino di Gallura is the local white wine — excellent with seafood.
Old Port Restaurants — Piazzetta
🐟 Seafood
The old port and Piazzetta keep the classic Sardinian tables — seafood, bottarga, Vermentino from the hills behind. In April, you can walk in without a reservation.
📍 Map
Vermentino di Gallura
🍷 Wine
Sardinia's finest white wine — DOCG status, grown in the granite hills behind Porto Cervo. Crisp, mineral, floral, with a slight bitter finish. Perfect with bottarga and fresh fish. Look for Capichera and Surrau labels.

Ortigia Market (Mercato di Ortigia)
🏪 Market🐟 Seafood
One of Sicily's best food markets — Via Trento and Via Emanuele de Benedictis in Ortigia. Fresh swordfish, tuna, sea urchins, blood oranges, capers, olives, pistachios. Mon–Sat morning. Arrive early for the best selection.
📍 Map
Neapolis Archaeological Park
🏭 History
Greek Theatre (5th century BC — one of the largest in the ancient world), the Ear of Dionysius (enormous limestone cave with extraordinary acoustics), and the Roman Amphitheatre. Short taxi ride from the port. Check if the Greek Theatre is open (closed mid-May to early July for drama festival).

Noto — Baroque Town (Day Trip)
📷 Photography
30 min by taxi — the finest Baroque town in Sicily, rebuilt entirely after the 1693 earthquake in golden limestone. The main street (Corso Vittorio Emanuele) is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Best in morning light. Caffè Sicilia (since 1892) is legendary for granita and pastries.

Sicilian Wines
🍷 Wine
For Holly: Etna Bianco (volcanic white from Mount Etna — mineral, complex, one of Italy's most exciting whites), Grillo (crisp Sicilian white), Catarratto (fresh, citrusy). Look for Benanti and Cornelissen labels for Etna Bianco.

Malta (Valletta)

⚓ Docks€ Euro

🏭Activities & Sights
Valletta — Europe's Smallest Capital
🚶 Walking📷 Photography
Built by the Knights of St. John after the Great Siege of 1565 — the entire city is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Walk Republic Street from the City Gate to Fort St. Elmo. The Upper Barrakka Gardens offer the finest view of the Grand Harbour.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
St. John's Co-Cathedral
🏭 History📷 Photography
The most ornate Baroque interior in the world — every inch of the floor is a marble tombstone of a Knight. Caravaggio's "The Beheading of Saint John the Baptist" (his largest work and only signed painting) is here. Book tickets at stjohnscocathedral.com. €15 entry.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
Three Cities — by Ferry
🏭 History📷 Photography
Birgu, Senglea, and Cospicua — the fortified cities across the Grand Harbour. Ferry from the Lower Barrakka Gardens (€1.50 each way). Birgu (Vittoriosa) has the Inquisitor's Palace and Fort St. Angelo. Far less touristy than Valletta — authentic Maltese life.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
Mdina — Silent City
📷 Photography
30 min by bus — Malta's ancient walled capital, population ~300. Car-free, honey-colored limestone, cathedral, and extraordinary views across the island. Best in early morning before day-trippers arrive. Combine with Rabat (catacombs) next door.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
Marsaxlokk Fish Market (Sunday)
🏪 Market🐟 Seafood
Malta's most famous market — Sunday morning fish market in the colorful fishing village of Marsaxlokk. Traditional luzzu fishing boats (painted with the Eye of Osiris) fill the harbor. Fresh fish, seafood, local produce. 30 min by bus from Valletta.
📍 Map
🍽Food & Wine
Holly-safe: Fresh fish (lampuki — dolphinfish, Malta's national fish), grilled octopus, seafood pasta, ftira (Maltese bread with tuna and capers — ask without cheese). English is widely spoken — just say "without garlic" and "no cheese". Maltese whites from Marsovin are improving.
Strait Street — Valletta Dining
🐟 Seafood
The old "Gut" — sailors' entertainment strip, now restaurants and bars. The area around St. John's Square has the best concentration of restaurants. Avoid tourist traps on Republic Street. Nenu the Artisan Baker for traditional Maltese ftira.
📍 Map

Venetian Harbour & Old Town
🚶 Walking📷 Photography
One of the most beautiful harbors in Greece — the Venetian lighthouse, the curved harbor wall, and the mix of Venetian, Ottoman, and Greek architecture. Chania Municipal Market (Agora)
🏪 Market✓ Holly
Cross-shaped covered market built in 1913 — Cretan olive oil (among the world's finest), thyme honey, dried herbs, fresh fish, local cheeses (Holly avoid), carob products. Mon–Sat 8 AM–3 PM. The best place to buy Cretan olive oil to take home.
📍 Map
Balos Lagoon & Gramvousa
✓ Holly📷 Photography
One of the most photographed beaches in Greece — turquoise lagoon, pink sand, Venetian castle on the island. Boat trip from Kissamos port (45 min west of Chania by taxi). Half-day. Book at Kissamos port or through the ship. April water is cool but the lagoon is spectacular.
📍 Map
Samaria Gorge (Full Day — opens May 1)
🚶 Walking
Europe's longest gorge — 16 km walk through the White Mountains to the Libyan Sea. Opens May 1 (check exact date for 2028). Bus from Chania to Xyloskalo (1 hr), walk the gorge (4–6 hrs), boat from Agia Roumeli to Hora Sfakion, bus back to Chania. Only if port time allows 10+ hours.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
🍽Food & Wine
Holly-safe: Cretan cuisine is Mediterranean at its finest. Fresh fish, grilled octopus, dakos (barley rusk with tomato — ask without cheese), fresh vegetables with olive oil. Say "χωρίς σκόρδο" (no garlic) and "χωρίς τυρί" (no cheese). Cretan olive oil is world-class — buy some at the Agora.
Cretan Olive Oil
✓ Holly
Crete produces some of the world's finest extra virgin olive oil — Kolymvari PDO from western Crete is particularly prized. Buy at the Agora market. Look for cold-pressed, early harvest varieties. Excellent to take home.
Cretan Wines
🍷 Wine
For Holly: Vidiano (crisp Cretan white — floral, mineral), Thrapsathiri (light, fresh white), Muscat of Spinas. Look for Lyrarakis and Douloufakis wineries. Avoid Kotsifali (red). Cretan wine is seriously underrated.

Arrive early morning to beat heat and crowds. Pre-book a private driver from Alexandria (3 hrs each way).

Bibliotheca Alexandrina
🏭 History
Stunning modern library and cultural center honoring the ancient Library of Alexandria — one of the greatest libraries of the ancient world. EGP 120 (~€2.50) entry. Allow 90 min. Best visited in the morning before tour groups arrive. 15 min by Uber from the port.

Citadel of Qaitbay
🏭 History📷 Photography
15th-century fortress built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria (one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World). EGP 100 (~€2) entry. Best in morning or late afternoon for light. The harbor views are excellent.

Catacombs of Kom el-Shoqafa
🏭 History
A fascinating blend of Egyptian and Greco-Roman funerary art in underground chambers — one of the Seven Wonders of the Middle Ages. EGP 100 (~€2) entry. 45 min. Underground site unaffected by weather. Combine with Pompey's Pillar nearby.

Stanley Bridge & Corniche Waterfront
🚶 Walking📷 Photography

Pergamon Acropolis & Asclepion (UNESCO)
🏭 History
One of antiquity's great cities — the Acropolis clings to a dramatic hilltop with the world's steepest ancient theater (capacity 10,000), while the Asclepion served as a celebrated healing center where Galen practiced medicine. A cable car (Teleferik) whisks you to the top. ~29 km / 40 min drive. 4–5 hours total. Negotiate a firm all-in taxi price including wait time (~€40–55 per taxi for a shared group).

🏪 Market✓ Holly
If your ship calls on Tuesday, walk straight to the weekly pazar — local olives, Bergama tulum cheese, spices, textiles, and fresh produce fill the stalls. Completely authentic and entirely free to browse. 10 min walk from pier. Tuesdays only. One of the most authentic experiences in town.

Çandarlı Castle
🏭 History📷 Photography
A beautifully preserved Genoese fortress perched over the sea in the coastal town of Çandarlı. Climb the ramparts for sweeping views of the Aegean — an easy half-day with a swim stop on the way back. 25 min south of Dikili by taxi. Small entry fee. 14th century.

Killik Bay & Thermal Mud Baths
✓ Holly
Drive south to Bademli's Killik Bay — often called the 'Maldives of the Aegean' for its turquoise water and distinctive golden sand. Natural white clay bubbles up nearby — rub it on your skin for a free spa session, then wash off in the sea. 20 min by taxi. Free beach access. 3–4 hours.

Ancient City of Troy (UNESCO)
🏭 History
The legendary city of Homer's Iliad — nine superimposed cities spanning 4,000 years of history. The ruins are subtle — read up before visiting or the layers won't mean much. A replica of the famous Trojan Horse stands at the entrance. 18 km from Kepez — taxi or pre-booked transport (~30 min). Allow 2 hours at the site. The Troy Museum nearby is excellent.

Gallipoli Peninsula — WWI Battlefields
🏭 History
Across the Dardanelles — a place of pilgrimage for Australians, New Zealanders, and Turks. ANZAC Cove, Lone Pine Cemetery, Chunuk Bair, and the Turkish memorials. Regular car ferries from Çanakkale (20–30 min crossing). A full-day commitment — only if port time allows 8+ hours. Deeply moving.

Çanakkale Waterfront & Naval Museum
🚶 Walking📷 Photography
The massive wooden horse used in the 2004 movie 'Troy' was gifted to the city and now stands proudly on the waterfront — a fun photo stop. The Çanakkale Naval Museum offers fascinating insights into the region's maritime and military history. The seafront promenade has fish restaurants, bars, and cafés.

🏭 History

Hagia Sophia
🏭 History📷 Photography
Built in 537 AD — for nearly 1,000 years the largest cathedral in the world. Now an active mosque. The interior is one of the most extraordinary spaces ever created — the floating dome, the golden mosaics, the sheer scale. Go at opening (9 AM) or after 3 PM to avoid the worst queues. Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered, women bring a scarf.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
Topkapi Palace
🏭 History
The administrative center of the Ottoman Empire for 400 years — the Harem, the Imperial Treasury (Topkapi Dagger, Spoonmaker's Diamond), and the Sacred Relics collection. €25 entry. Book online to skip queues. Allow 3 hours. The views of the Bosphorus from the palace gardens are extraordinary.
📍 Map🤖 Ask Historian
Grand Bazaar (Kapaliçarşı)
🏪 Market📷 Photography
Founded in 1461 by Sultan Mehmed II — one of the oldest and largest covered markets in the world. 61 covered streets, 4,000+ shops, 30,000+ m². Open 8:30 AM–7 PM daily. Closed Sundays and public holidays. Haggling is expected — start at 40–50% of the first price. Ceramics, textiles, spices, jewelry, leather.

Spice Bazaar (Mısır Çarşısı)
🏪 Market✓ Holly
Built in 1660 — the center of the spice trade between Istanbul and the East. L-shaped building, 85 shops. Open 8 AM–7:30 PM daily. Closed Sundays and public holidays. Saffron, dried fruits, Turkish delight, olive oil soaps, spices. Excellent for Holly's food preferences — dried fruits, olive oils, spices.

Galataport — Istanbul Modern & Waterfront
🚶 Walking📷 Photography
The ship docks at Galataport — a 1.2 km car-free waterfront promenade in Karaköy. Istanbul Modern (Renzo Piano building, 2023) is steps from the ship — Turkey's finest modern art museum (~750 TRY entry). The Kiliç Ali Pasha Mosque (1580–1587, Mimar Sinan) is on the promenade. Walk the promenade for Bosphorus views — the Historic Peninsula on one side, the strait on the other.

Bosphorus Ferry
✓ Holly📷 Photography
Take a public ferry from Karaköy or Eminönü across to the Asian side (Kadıköy or Üsküdar) — one of the world's great short ferry rides. ~€0.50 each way. The view of the Historic Peninsula from the water is extraordinary. Kadıköy has an excellent food market and local restaurants.

Kadıköy Market (Asian Side)
🏪 Market✓ Holly
Take the ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side — the Kadıköy Produce Market (Moda Caddesi area) is one of Istanbul's best food markets. Fresh fish, dried fruits, olives, spices, local cheeses (Holly avoid), fresh produce. More local and less touristy than the Spice Bazaar.

Turkish Wines
🍷 Wine
For Holly: Turkish whites from the Aegean region — Urla Winery Sauvignon Blanc, Chamlija Sauvignon Blanc, Kavaklidere Egeblend (white). Turkey's wine industry is improving rapidly. Wine shops in Karaköy have excellent selections. Avoid Bogazkere and Okuzgozu (reds).
